## Transcription rules

Pages 2–6 list prices in thousands of rupiah. Pages 7–10 give full rupiah amounts for whole cakes and celebration products. The matching slice/whole-cake amounts corroborate the scale (for example Carrot Cake 49/490 and whole cake Rp490,000).

- Pages 2–3: viennoiserie. Croissant variants are grouped into a family with their individual prices. Other items retain individual product pages.
- Page 4: tarte/quiche and choux. Choux flavors are variants with their published prices.
- Pages 5 and 7: slice/whole-cake prices and dimensions. Unpublished dimensions are not invented. Cashew Gianduja's 14cm price is taken from page 7.
- Pages 6–8: bread, dessert portions, rectangular whole cakes, 14cm round flavors, and 20cm round tiramisu. The Rp500,000 20cm size is offered only for Tiramisu because that is the explicit published label.
- Page 9: Tiramisu Tower Rp1,500,000. Its printed contact differs from the main number; the website consistently uses the official product-ordering number from the bio and other menu pages.
- Page 10: Dubai Chewy Celebration Package Rp545,000; 15 pieces with candle, topper, and greeting card.
- Mix Cake: the menu says price depends on chosen flavors, so it is an inquiry.
- Hampers: the linked priced brochure is for Lebaran 2026, not an established current November offer. The website asks for a current quote rather than selling an expired seasonal package.

The menu repeatedly states prices exclude government tax (10%). The website reflects that published rate in estimates, without asserting a separate tax-law conclusion. It asks staff to confirm the final total. No delivery or unverified service fee is added.

The menu says products not on display should be ordered one day earlier, and sourdough two days earlier. Because the site has no live display inventory, its priced catalog is a pre-order request channel using those lead times. Same-day display-stock requests go directly to WhatsApp. Pickup times are preferences, never reserved slots.

## Images

Existing wordmark, avatar, tiramisu, pastry, and floral-cake references came from the publicly linked brand pages. Transparent cake photos in `public/images/menu/` were decoded from embedded images on page 7 of the pastry menu, including the PDF's alpha masks, and converted to WebP. Product-image mapping was visually checked against the labeled page.

These are actual source images, not generated representations of different bakery products. Use higher-resolution originals when the bakery supplies them. Retain the bakery's permission for public web use of its branding and photography when publishing publicly.
